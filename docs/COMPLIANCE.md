# Compliance Controls

ReviewOrbit supplies configurable technical controls and does not provide legal conclusions. Each business must confirm its lawful basis and messaging obligations.

Consent is business- and channel-specific with status, source, timestamp, disclosure version, and evidence. Contact data received from Square does not create or replace consent. STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT are normalized case-insensitively; an opt-out updates consent, adds suppression, cancels future messages, and creates an audit entry. Reappearance through a POS never resubscribes a number; lawful resubscription is an explicit privileged audited workflow.

Scheduling uses location/business IANA time zones, quiet hours, frequency limits, sender identity, and configurable support/address text. Suppression is checked immediately before provider submission as well as during initial eligibility.

The system requests honest feedback and redirects all eligible recipients to the same configured Google review destination. There is no rating interstitial, sentiment route, incentive, fabricated identity, or positive-only gate. UI and reports say **Review link clicked**, not **Review submitted**.

Businesses receive configurable export, deletion/anonymization, retention, and privacy settings. Personal media and request metadata expire; required consent/audit evidence may be retained under documented policy with access controls.
